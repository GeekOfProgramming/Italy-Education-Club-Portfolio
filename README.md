## About The Project
As the Founder and Frontend Developer, I architected and managed the core platform for Italy Education Club, a system that successfully processed over 500 client applications.

## My Role & Responsibilities
* **Frontend Development:** Developed the entire client-facing platform and internal dashboard (UI), focusing on the client acquisition and application-processing flow.
* **Project Management:** Designed and implemented structured digital workflows, reducing application processing time by 20%.
* **Leadership:** Scaled the operation from zero to a 12-person team and 500+ clients.
